Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of how this app handles your information.

What we keep

  • Your username, and your password as a one-way scrambled value (we cannot read your password).
  • The expenses you add: date, amount, category, shop name and items.
    • {% if ocr_enabled %}
  • Bill photos you upload, which are read by the app to find the amount, date and items.
    • {% endif %}
  • Profile answers you choose to give (budget, goals, habits). All of them are optional.
  • The money tips that were made for you.

Where it is kept

In a cloud database (MongoDB Atlas) and the app is hosted on a cloud server. Connections use HTTPS.

AI money tips

Only when you tick the box and press the button, a short summary is sent to an AI provider to write your tips: totals per category, number of entries, spending by weekday, a few item names, today's budget numbers and your profile answers. We do not send your username or name, shop names, bill photos or bill text. The provider's own terms apply to what you send; on some free plans a provider may use submitted text to improve its products. If you do not want this, do not use the tips page.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We never ask for card numbers, bank passwords or UPI PINs. Please never type them anywhere in this app.

Your choices

  • Download all your expenses any time from your profile page.
  • Delete your account and all your data any time from your profile page. It is removed straight away.
  • You can leave any profile question empty.

Keeping data

Your data is kept until you delete your account.

Children

This app is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Security

We use hashed passwords, HTTPS and limits on repeated login attempts. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: lebika2810@gmail.com

We may update this policy; the latest version is always on this page.
